## 🧮 My favorite math formulas
Mathematics is full of elegant ideas!
### 1. Quadratic equation

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

This formula is used to find the roots of a quadratic equation. Here, $a$, $b$, and $c$ are coefficients, and $x$ represents the unknown variable.

### 2. Pythagorean theorem

$$
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
$$

This theorem describes the relationship between the sides of a right triangle. Here, $a$ and $b$ are the legs, and $c$ is the hypotenuse.

### 3. Euler's identity

$$
e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
$$

This identity connects five fundamental mathematical constants: $e$, $i$, $\pi$, $1$, and $0$.

### 4. Definite integral

$$
\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a)
$$

This formula calculates the definite integral of a function. Here, $f(x)$ is the integrand, $a$ and $b$ are integration limits, and $F(x)$ is an antiderivative of $f(x)$.

### 5. Sum of a sequence

$$
S_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

This formula calculates the sum of the first $n$ natural numbers. Here, $S_n$ is the sum, and $k$ is the summation index.

### 6. System of linear equations

$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + z = 6 \\
2x - y + z = 3 \\
x + 2y - z = 2
\end{cases}
$$

This system represents three linear equations with three unknowns: $x$, $y$, and $z$. The goal is to find values that satisfy all three equations simultaneously.

### 7. Identity matrix

$$
I_N =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1 & \cdots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

An identity matrix is a square matrix with ones on the main diagonal and zeros elsewhere. Here, $N$ represents the number of rows and columns.
### 8. Greek letters

$$
\alpha + \beta = \gamma,\quad
\delta + \epsilon = \zeta,\quad
\eta + \theta = \lambda
$$

Greek letters are widely used in mathematics to represent variables, parameters, and constants.
